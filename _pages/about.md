My name is Sarah Quỳnh Giang Hồ. Born and raised in sunny southern California, USA to Vietnamese immigrants, I received my bachelor's in Civil & Environmental Engineering at the [University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA)](https://www.ucla.edu/), and worked for the [City of Los Angeles Bureau of Public Works - Bureau of Engineering](https://engineering.lacity.gov/) for about a year and a half. Seeking a bit of change in my life, I took a leap of faith in 2019 and moved to Munich, Germany, where I earned my master's in Environmental Engineering from the [Technical University of Munich (TUM)](https://www.tum.de/) with a focus in hydrology. I then moved to Karlsruhe, Germany in 2022 for a doctorate at the [Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT)](https://www.kit.edu/)'s [Institute for Water and Environment - Hydrology](https://www.iwu.kit.edu/hyd/english/index.php), which I successfully defended in January of 2026. Since then, I have been working at KIT as a postdoc at the Institute of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing.
