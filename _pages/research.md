My primary research interest is drought: growing up in drought-stricken, wildfire-ridden California, it's always been a topic on the news and particularly in my coursework. When I moved to Germany, a water-rich country, I thought I wouldn't need to think about it anymore. Of course, [this was](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/news/why-the-rhine-drought-is-bad-news-for-germany-s-recovery/ar-AA29MAl7) [absolutely not](https://www.dw.com/en/germany-sees-record-breaking-heat-drought-and-sun-in-2022/a-64248580) [the case](https://www.zeit.de/wissen/umwelt/2020-04/trockenheit-duerre-deutschland-klimaforscher-mojib-latif-klimaschutz), particularly as our climate changes rapidly in response to human activity. With my research, I hope to help society adequately prepare for the consequences of drought.
